Durable sessions

Context that outlives a request, a worker, and a deploy.

Replayable events

An ordered, append only history you can resume from any cursor.

Human approval

Runs pause, release the worker, and wake when a decision lands.

Budgets

Steps, tools, tokens, cost, and time, carried across retries.

Idempotent tools

A ledger that keeps a retry from firing a side effect twice.

Recovery

A lost worker is noticed, and the work retried as a new attempt.
